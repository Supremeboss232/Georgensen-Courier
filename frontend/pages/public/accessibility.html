Accessibility Statement (WCAG 2.1 AA & AODA)

Georgensen Courier Inc. is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities in compliance with the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA), ADA Title III, and WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines.

Measures to Support Accessibility

  • Keyboard-navigable forms, tracking timelines, and quote calculators.
  • ARIA labels and semantic HTML landmarks across customer, partner, and public portals.
  • High-contrast typography and dark-mode support.

If you experience any accessibility barriers, please contact our Accessibility Coordinator via our support portal or email accessibility@georgensencourier.com.
