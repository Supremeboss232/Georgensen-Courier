Data Processing Addendum (Enterprise DPA)

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Master Logistics Services Agreement between Georgensen Courier Inc. ("Processor") and Enterprise Customers ("Controller") pursuant to GDPR Article 28, UK GDPR, and PIPEDA.

1. Scope of Processing

Georgensen Courier processes sender and recipient contact data, customs declarations, GPS coordinates, and proof-of-delivery signatures strictly for the fulfillment of logistics and courier shipments.

2. Technical & Organizational Security Measures

  • TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and immutable Chain-of-Custody shipment audit logs.
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for cross-border customs and hub transfers.
