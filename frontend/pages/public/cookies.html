Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Georgensen Courier uses strictly necessary session storage and local storage tokens to authenticate users and remember language/currency preferences.

Categories of Storage Used

  • Strictly Necessary Authentication Tokens (authToken, refresh_token): Required to maintain secure JWT sessions across Customer, Partner, and Admin portals.
  • Functional Preferences (preferences, appLanguage): Remembers dark-mode, language (EN/FR), and regional currency selections.
