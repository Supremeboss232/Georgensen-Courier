California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA) Notice

This notice supplements the Georgensen Courier Privacy Policy and applies to California residents.

Your CCPA Rights

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of categories and specific pieces of personal information collected regarding your shipments and account.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information via our Data Deletion Portal (subject to customs and tax record retention laws).
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing: Georgensen Courier does not sell personal information to third parties.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.
